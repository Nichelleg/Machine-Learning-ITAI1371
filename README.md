## Personal Reflection

Introduction to Machine Learning was an important step in my transition into the Applied AI & Robotics program.

The course helped connect my previous experience analyzing business and real estate data with the technical methods used to develop predictive systems.

One of my most important takeaways was understanding that machine learning involves much more than choosing an algorithm and running code.

The reliability of a model depends on the quality of its input data, the appropriateness of its evaluation methods, and the ability to interpret its outputs within the context of the problem being addressed.

The midterm and final projects provided opportunities to apply these concepts through practical Python exercises.

This foundation supported my subsequent coursework in Natural Language Processing, Deep Learning, Data Science, and Computer Vision.
